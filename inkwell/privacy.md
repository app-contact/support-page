# Privacy Policy - Inkwell

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Introduction

Inkwell ("the App") is a Confluence Cloud application developed by middlecore. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App stores and how we handle it.

## Data Collection

### What We Store

To provide section tracking, the App stores the following data in Atlassian Forge hosted storage:

- **Section data**: section titles, instructions, statuses, order, due dates, timestamps, and review comments
- **User references**: Atlassian account IDs of assignees, reviewers, and section creators/editors
- **Cached display names**: the display names of assignees, reviewers, and section creators, cached for display purposes
- **Page metadata**: page IDs, space IDs, and aggregate completion statistics

All of this data remains within Atlassian's cloud infrastructure at all times.

### What We Do Not Collect

- No email addresses, passwords, or credentials
- No Confluence page body content (the App reads page information only to render its features in real-time)
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry data sent to external services

## Data Storage

All data is stored in **Atlassian Forge hosted storage**, which runs entirely within Atlassian's secure cloud infrastructure. Data is encrypted at rest and never leaves Atlassian's environment. The App makes no outbound network requests to external servers.

## Personal Data Reporting (Atlassian User Privacy Guidelines)

In accordance with Atlassian's user privacy guidelines for Forge apps, the App periodically reports the Atlassian account IDs it stores to Atlassian:

- If an account's profile has been **updated**, the App refreshes its cached display name
- If an account has been **closed**, the App erases that user's personal data from its storage

## Data Retention

- Data is retained while the App is installed on your site
- When the App is uninstalled, all app data is automatically deleted by the Forge platform after 28 days
- Personal data of closed Atlassian accounts is erased as described above

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

## Sub-processors

The App does not use any sub-processors. All processing occurs within Atlassian's Forge infrastructure.

## Security

Inkwell is built on Atlassian Forge, which runs entirely within Atlassian's secure cloud infrastructure.

Key security features:
- Backend code (resolvers) executes within Atlassian's serverless infrastructure
- Frontend runs in a sandboxed iframe within your browser
- No external network requests are made
- Data is encrypted at rest and in transit

See our [Security Policy](security.md) for details.

## GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA):

- **Data Minimization**: We store only the data required to provide section tracking (account IDs, display names, and section metadata)
- **No Data Transfer**: No data is transferred outside of Atlassian's infrastructure
- **Right to Erasure**: Personal data of closed Atlassian accounts is erased automatically via Atlassian's personal data reporting cycle. For other erasure requests, contact us at contact@middle-core.com
- **Right to Information**: You may contact us at contact@middle-core.com for any privacy-related inquiries

## CCPA Compliance

For California residents:

- We do not sell personal information
- We store only the minimal personal information described above (account IDs and display names)
- For questions or requests, contact us at contact@middle-core.com


## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
